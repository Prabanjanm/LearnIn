Privacy Policy

LearnIn does not require students to create an account to browse papers, practice questions, or take mock tests. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Basic, non-identifying usage data (such as search terms) may be logged to help us improve the content we provide. Question papers, answer keys, and study materials are hosted on Google Drive and served through direct links.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@learnin.app.
