Privacy Policy

This page describes what LearnIn actually collects and why. Browsing exams, previous-year papers, and blog content, and downloading question papers or answer keys, does not require an account and is not tied to your identity.

Account information

Creating a free account to track your mock-test results requires only an email address, a password, and an optional name. Your password is never stored in readable form - only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is kept, which cannot be reversed back into your original password.

Test and practice data

If you take a mock test or practice questions while logged in, we store which questions you attempted, the answers you selected, and your resulting score/accuracy, so your dashboard can show your history and identify subjects you may want to practice more. If you use these features without logging in, the same data is stored against an anonymous session rather than your account.

Usage logs

Search terms entered on the site are logged (along with how many results they returned) purely to help us understand what students are looking for and improve our content coverage. This is not linked to your account.

Cookies

LearnIn uses a session cookie only to keep you logged in after you sign in (and a separate one for admin staff managing content). We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it before those cookies are set.

Third-party services

Question papers, answer keys, and other downloadable files are stored on Google Drive and served through Google's infrastructure. Google's own privacy policy applies to that file storage/delivery. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention and your choices

We keep account and test-history data for as long as your account exists. To request a copy of your data, ask a question about this policy, or request that your account and associated data be deleted, contact us at support@learnin.app.
