About LearnIn

LearnIn is a free platform for government exam preparation, starting with GATE and expanding to UPSC, SSC, TNPSC, RRB, Bank, NEET, JEE, CAT, TANCET and more.

We bring together previous year question papers, subject-wise practice questions, mock tests, and study materials in one place so students can prepare efficiently without hunting across dozens of scattered sources.
